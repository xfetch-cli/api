<h2 id="before-opening-an-issue" align="center">Before Opening an Issue</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Confirm which crate (<code>xfetch-plugin-api</code>, <code>xfetch-extension-api</code>, <code>xfetch-effect-api</code>) and version you use.</li>
  <li>Include a minimal reproduction (crate version, protocol message, expected vs actual).</li>
  <li>Check the <a href="./docs/protocol.md">protocol reference</a> before reporting wire-format issues.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="response-expectations" align="center">Response Expectations</h2>

<p>
  Maintainers triage reports based on impact and reproducibility. Clear, minimal reproductions are resolved faster.
</p>
